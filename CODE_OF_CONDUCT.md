# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Debate technical choices with evidence and assume good intent where reasonable. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing personal or confidential information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate and applied consistently. This applies to project issues, pull requests and other spaces managed by the project.

For a confidential report, ask a maintainer for a private contact channel without posting the incident details publicly. The public repository must identify its moderation contact before opening community participation. Do not include private business records or credentials in a report.
